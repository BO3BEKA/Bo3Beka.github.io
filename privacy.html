Jarvis privacy policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission, Jarvis reads and changes the owner's own Google Tasks. That data is stored only on the owner's computer, is used only to show and manage those tasks, and is never sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

The owner can remove Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
